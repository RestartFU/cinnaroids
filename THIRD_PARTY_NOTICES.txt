Cinnabar clicker — Third-party notices
====================================

This application uses GPUI and other Rust packages. Its own source and
independently drawn branding assets are covered by the root LICENSE.txt.
Upstream package licenses and notices are retained under
licenses/<name-version>/. Files are copied unchanged from exact locally
downloaded crate versions. Where a crate omitted standalone license files,
some were retrieved from the exact published crate VCS commit; their source
URLs are recorded in the corresponding UPSTREAM_SOURCE.txt.

This inventory covers every external package in Cargo.lock, including
optional dependencies, build tools, and dependencies for other platforms.
An entry in this inventory does not imply that package is linked into the
Windows executable. Refer to Cargo.toml for enabled application features.
License expressions, authors, and repositories below come from each exact
published crate Cargo.toml; copyright holders are not inferred from authors.
