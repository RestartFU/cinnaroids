Cinnabar clicker visual references
================================

The mark.svg and cinnabar-mark.svg assets are independently drawn C monograms
created for this app. They do not contain copied logos or pixel data.
The cinnabar-icon.png and cinnabar.ico files are raster conversions of the
independently drawn cinnabar-mark.svg. The ICO contains 16, 32, 48, 64, 128
and 256 pixel images.

The minimize.svg, maximize.svg,
restore.svg, and close.svg files are original 16x16 line icons for the regular
top-right window controls. They contain no copied platform artwork.

Design inspiration: the vermilion and coral palette of the Cinnabar project's
red title art and cube icon, combined with the user's initial dark sidebar
screenshot. Version 1.4.0 uses neutral charcoal or white surfaces, a single
coral accent, two tabs and regular top-right window controls. Backgrounds are
rendered by GPUI with native Windows acrylic. No Apple artwork or system
assets are included.

art generated for Cinnabar. No license granting reuse of those branding assets
was found, so neither is included in this distribution. No game resources are
included.

This is an independent external clicker app. It is not the Bedrock game client
distributed by bedrock-mc/cinnabar and is not affiliated with its maintainers.
