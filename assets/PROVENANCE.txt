Cinnaroids visual references
===========================

The mark.svg and cinnaroids-mark.svg assets are independently drawn C
monograms created for this app. They contain no copied logos or pixel data.
The cinnaroids-icon.png and cinnaroids.ico files are raster conversions of
cinnaroids-mark.svg. The ICO contains 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixel images.

The minimize.svg, maximize.svg, restore.svg, and close.svg files are original
16x16 line icons for the regular top-right window controls.

The neutral charcoal and white launcher surfaces use a coral accent with a
native Windows backdrop. No Apple artwork or system assets are included.

Design inspiration: https://github.com/bedrock-mc/cinnabar/
Reference commit: bcf245974d6ba81f093141898defb5fb2c13b348
Reference files: assets/branding/title.png, packaging/icons/cinnabar.svg,
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

No license granting reuse of the referenced branding assets was found, so
they are not included. No game resources are committed to this repository.
Cinnaroids is independent of the Cinnabar client and its maintainers.
